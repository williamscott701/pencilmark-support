Privacy Policy

Pencilmark: Sudoku. Last updated 12 November 2026.

No account, no advertising, no tracking. To learn which levels, hints and screens work, Pencilmark sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase. They are not linked to you, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics

While the setting is on, the app sends short events about how the game is used, for example:

  • a level started, finished or left, with its number, chapter, time, ticks, hints and mistakes;
  • the daily challenge played and the length of the streak;
  • a hint asked for, and the technique it named;
  • the trial screen or plan screen opened, the plan picked, and whether a purchase or restore went through (the product and the price the App Store reports);
  • a setting changed, such as sound, music, haptics or appearance.

Google Analytics adds general device information: the device model, iOS and app version, language and an approximate country.

Never sent: your name, email address or any account, a user ID, the advertising identifier (the module that reads it isn't in the app, and the app never asks to track you), the vendor identifier, your contacts, photos or precise location. The statistics are not used for advertising: ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are denied everywhere, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is denied too, so only identifier-free pings are sent there.

Google processes these statistics for the developer under the Firebase privacy terms and Google's privacy policy, and keeps them for a limited time.

Turning them off

Open Settings in the app and switch off Share anonymous usage statistics under Privacy. Nothing more is sent from that moment, including on later launches, until you switch it back on.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles in progress, level progress, statistics and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are removed if you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud, they are part of that backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The 15-day trial, the subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app asks the App Store only what this Apple Account owns and whether a subscription is active. The developer never receives your name, email address or payment details.

Network

The app connects to the App Store for purchases and, while usage statistics are on, to Google Analytics for Firebase. The levels and the daily challenge are built into the app; nothing is downloaded.

Children

Pencilmark doesn't ask anyone's age or name, and nothing it sends identifies a person, child or adult. The anonymous statistics can be turned off in Settings.
