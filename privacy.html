Privacy Policy

Pencilmark: Sudoku. Last updated 6 October 2026.

Pencilmark collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it does not send anything about you or your games to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles in progress, statistics and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are removed if you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud, they are part of that backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The 7-day trial and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app asks the App Store only whether this Apple Account owns them. The developer never receives your name, email address or payment details.

Network

The only network connection the app makes is to the App Store for purchases. The daily puzzle is built into the app; nothing is downloaded.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.
