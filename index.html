Pencilmark

Classic sudoku on graph paper, for iPhone and iPad.

A path of 150 levels in 17 chapters teaches one technique at a time, from the last empty square to unique rectangles. The first levels take a minute or two; each chapter thins the clues. Every level earns up to three ticks, and is the same puzzle for everyone.

A new daily challenge arrives each day, gentle on Monday and Fiendish on Sunday, with a streak for the days in a row. Free play offers five grades, from Easy (singles only) to Fiendish (W-Wings, colouring and unique rectangles).

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is graded by the techniques a person needs to solve it. Pencil marks tidy themselves, and hints explain the next technique in a sentence and point at the squares.

No ads, no tracking, no account and no data collected. Your games and statistics stay on your device.

Price

Pencilmark is free for 10 days, with everything included. After that, the whole game locks until you choose a plan:

  • Pencilmark Notebook, Monthly: US$0.99 a month, an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Pencilmark Notebook, Yearly: US$4.99 a year, an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Pencilmark Notebook, Lifetime: US$7.99, paid once, with Family Sharing.

Prices vary by country; the app shows yours before you buy. Starting the trial costs nothing and nothing renews by itself. Your levels, streak and statistics are kept while the game is locked, and come back as soon as you choose a plan. There are no ads, ever. The full terms are on the Terms of Use page.

Support

Questions, a puzzle that seems wrong, or an idea? Write to williamscott701@gmail.com. If you can, include the grade and what the hint said.

Restoring a plan: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Family Sharing members get the lifetime purchase too.

Cancelling a subscription: open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pencilmark. Subscribers also find Manage subscription in Pencilmark's own Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal.
