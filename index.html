Pencilmark

Learn sudoku one technique at a time, in soft pencil, on iPhone and iPad.

A path of 150 levels in 17 chapters teaches one technique at a time, from the last empty square to unique rectangles. Early levels are quick, with most squares printed; each chapter thins the clues. Every level earns up to three ticks, and is the same puzzle for everyone.

A daily grid climbs through the week, from Easy on Monday to Fiendish on Sunday, with a streak for the days in a row. Every seventh day in a row banks a shield (up to two) that covers a missed day, and a reminder can come at the hour you pick, only on days still unsolved. Single puzzles come in five grades, from Easy (singles only) to Fiendish (W-Wings, colouring and unique rectangles).

A solved level, the daily result (never its digits), a streak milestone, a new best time or your progress can be shared as a card sized for a status or story, or for a post.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is graded by the techniques a person needs to solve it. Pencil marks tidy themselves, and hints explain the next technique in a sentence and point at the squares.

No ads, no tracking and no account. Anonymous usage statistics help show which levels and hints work; you can turn them off in Settings. Your games and statistics stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Price

Start the 15-day Trial on the first screen and every level, grid and hint is free to play for 15 days. After that, every puzzle locks until you choose a plan:

  • Pencilmark Notebook, Monthly: US$0.99 a month, an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Pencilmark Notebook, Yearly: US$4.99 a year, an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Pencilmark Notebook, Lifetime: US$7.99, paid once, with Family Sharing.

Prices vary by country; the app shows yours before you buy. Starting the trial costs nothing and nothing renews by itself. Your levels, streak and statistics wait for you while the game is locked, and come back as soon as you choose a plan. There are no ads, ever. The full terms are on the Terms of Use page.

Support

Questions, a puzzle that seems wrong, or an idea? Write to williamscott701@gmail.com. If you can, include the grade and what the hint said.

Restoring a plan: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Family Sharing members get the lifetime purchase too.

Cancelling a subscription: open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Pencilmark. Subscribers also find Manage subscription in Pencilmark's own Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal.
