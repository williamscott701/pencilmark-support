Pencilmark

Classic sudoku on graph paper, for iPhone and iPad.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is graded by the techniques a person needs to solve it, from Easy (singles only) to Fiendish (W-Wings, colouring and unique rectangles). Pencil marks tidy themselves, hints explain the technique in a sentence and point at the squares, and a new daily puzzle arrives each day.

No ads, no tracking, no account and no data collected. Your games and statistics stay on your device.

Price

Pencilmark starts with a 7-day trial of everything. After that, starting or continuing a puzzle needs a single one-time purchase of the full game (US$0.99, or the equivalent in your country, shown in the app before you buy). There is no subscription. Statistics, settings and the rules stay open without it.

Support

Questions, a puzzle that seems wrong, or an idea? Write to williamscott701@gmail.com. If you can, include the grade and what the hint said.

Restoring the purchase: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Family Sharing members get the full game too.
