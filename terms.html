Pencilmark is licensed to you under Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the Apple EULA). These terms add a plain description of how the free trial, the subscriptions and the lifetime purchase work.

The 10-day trial

  • The trial is an in-app item called "10-day Trial" with a price of zero. Starting it costs nothing, needs no payment and does not start a subscription.
  • It lasts 10 days (240 hours) from the moment it is first started on your Apple Account, and includes everything: all 150 levels, the daily challenge, every grade, hints and statistics. Reinstalling the app or restoring purchases does not start it again.
  • When the 10 days end, nothing is charged and nothing renews. The whole game then locks: no puzzle can be started or continued until you subscribe or buy the lifetime purchase. Your levels, streak, statistics and puzzles in progress are kept, and statistics, settings and the rules stay available.

The subscriptions

  • "Pencilmark Notebook, Monthly" (US$0.99 a month) and "Pencilmark Notebook, Yearly" (US$4.99 a year) are auto-renewing subscriptions in one subscription group. Prices vary by country; the app shows the price for yours before you buy. Neither includes a free trial or introductory offer.
  • Each unlocks the whole game, including the daily challenge, for as long as it is active.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same price, unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription in Pencilmark's Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period already paid for.
  • When a subscription ends without a lifetime purchase, the game locks again as described above, and your progress is kept.

The lifetime purchase

  • "Pencilmark Notebook, Lifetime" is a single, non-consumable purchase (US$7.99, or the price the App Store shows for your country). It unlocks the whole game permanently for the Apple Account that buys it. Nothing renews.
  • Family Sharing is supported for the lifetime purchase.
  • If you also have a subscription, the lifetime purchase does not cancel it; cancel it in Settings as described above.

Payments, refunds and restoring

  • Payment, refunds and receipts are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com; a refunded purchase or subscription no longer unlocks the game.
  • You can restore your plan on another device signed in to the same Apple Account with Restore purchases in the app.
  • There are no ads and no other purchases in the app.
