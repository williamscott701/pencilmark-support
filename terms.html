The 7-day trial

  • The trial is an in-app item called "7-day Trial" with a price of zero. Starting it costs nothing and does not start a subscription.

  • When the 7 days end, nothing is charged. Starting or continuing a puzzle then needs the one-time purchase below. Statistics, settings and the rules stay available.

The one-time purchase

  • "Pencilmark full game" is a single, non-consumable purchase (US$0.99, or the price the App Store shows for your country). It unlocks the full game permanently for the Apple Account that buys it.
  • There is no subscription and nothing renews. There are no other purchases in the app.
  • Family Sharing is supported for the full game.
  • Payment, refunds and receipts are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com; a refunded purchase no longer unlocks the game.
  • You can restore the purchase on another device signed in to the same Apple Account with Restore purchases in the app.
